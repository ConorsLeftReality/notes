Traffic Lights (Port 01)

The traffic lights are on Port 01. Each bit of AL turns a light on (1) or off (0). The last 2 bits aren't connected

  • 84 - red, green
  • 48 - orange, orange
  • 30 - green, red

Put the value in AL, then out 01

Delays

Without a delay the lights change too fast to see. A delay is just a loop that counts up and does nothing

delay.asm
delay1:
  mov bl, 0
  loop1:
     inc bl
     cmp bl, 20     ; has bl reached 20?
     jnz loop1      ; if not, keep looping
     jmp continue1  ; otherwise jump back

The problem: every delay needs its own copy of this code and its own jump labels. There has to be a better way... subroutines
