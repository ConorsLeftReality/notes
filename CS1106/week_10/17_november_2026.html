ER Diagrams

An Entity-Relationship diagram is a blueprint of the database, drawn before you build it. Good design = no redundancy, data integrity, efficient

Trick for finding entities: the important nouns in the description are usually entities, the verbs are relationships. E.g. "students enrol in modules taught by lecturers" (a grade is an attribute of an enrolment, not an entity)

Chen Notation

  • Entity - rectangle (weak entity = double rectangle, needs another entity to be identified)
  • Attribute - oval. Key attributes are underlined, derived attributes (e.g. total price) are dashed, composite attributes (e.g. full name) branch into smaller ovals
  • Relationship - diamond, labelled with a verb

Cardinality

  • 1:1 - one user has one settings record
  • 1:N - one customer places many orders
  • M:N - students and modules

Most relationships are binary (2 entities), some are ternary (3), e.g. Doctor treats Patient using Medicine
