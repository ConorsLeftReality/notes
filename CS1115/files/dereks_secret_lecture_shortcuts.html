What's going on

Derek's slides are made with reveal.js (version 4.5) and a chalkboard plugin, which come with a load of keyboard shortcuts nobody tells you about

Click on the slides first so the keyboard goes to them, then press...

The one to remember

Press ? on any slide to bring up the full list of shortcuts. Everything below is from that list

Moving Around

  • N or SPACE - next slide
  • P or Shift + SPACE - previous slide
  • ← / H - left, → / L - right
  • ↑ / K - up, ↓ / J - down (vim keys!)
  • Alt + arrow - move without stepping through the bits that appear one at a time
  • Shift + arrow - jump to the first / last slide
  • G - jump to a slide, type its number

Viewing

  • ESC or O - overview of every slide, great for finding something fast
  • F - fullscreen
  • . - pause (blacks out the screen)
  • S - speaker notes view (opens a popup with the current and next slide, a timer, and any notes Derek left)
Note

The help list says B is pause too, but the chalkboard plugin takes over B, so use . to pause

Drawing on the Slides

This is the chalkboard plugin. Good for annotating while you revise

  • C - draw straight on top of the slide (notes canvas)
  • B - open a blank whiteboard to draw on
  • Left click and drag - draw
  • Right click (or middle click) and drag - erase
  • X / Y - next / previous pen colour
  • DEL - clear the drawings on this slide
  • BACKSPACE - reset all your drawings
  • D - download your drawings (as a .json file)

There are also little pen and board buttons at the bottom left of the slides that do the same as C and B

Heads up

Drawings aren't saved anywhere unless you press D, so they're gone when you reload the page

URL Tricks

  • The slide number is in the URL, e.g. WD1_20.html#/12, so you can link straight to a slide
  • Add ?print-pdf to the end of the URL (before the #), e.g. WD1_20.html?print-pdf, then print the page (Ctrl + P, Save as PDF) to get every slide as a PDF
